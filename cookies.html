Cookies Policy

How and why we use cookies and similar technologies on the Zambia Lobito Railway website.

Last updated: May 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve their performance and remember user preferences. We also use local storage, a related browser technology that allows websites to keep small pieces of information on your device.

2. The cookies and local storage we use

This site uses a deliberately small set of essential technologies. We do not currently run advertising trackers, social media pixels or third-party analytics on the site.

NameTypePurposeDuration
zlr_lang Local storage Remembers your language preference (English or Portuguese) so the site loads in your chosen language on return visits. Persistent until cleared by you
zlr_cookies_ok Local storage Records that you have acknowledged the cookies banner so we don't show it again. Persistent until cleared by you

3. Server logs

In addition to the items above, our hosting provider records standard request information (such as your IP address, browser type, time of request) in server logs for security and diagnostic purposes. These are not stored in your browser and are kept only for a limited period.

4. Managing cookies and local storage

You can clear or block cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings. The links below explain how:

  • Google Chrome
  • Apple Safari
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

If you clear our local storage entries, you may need to set your language preference again on your next visit, and the cookies banner will reappear.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently revised. For information about how we handle personal data more broadly, see our Media Pack Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

For any questions about this Cookies Policy, please email info@zambialobitorail.com.
