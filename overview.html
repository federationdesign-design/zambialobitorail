Project Overview

Completing the
Trans-African Corridor

A c.830km greenfield rail line connecting Chingola, Zambia to Luacano, Angola - bridging the Benguela line to the Zambia Railways network and unlocking Atlantic and Indian Ocean trade.

Strategic Context

One corridor. Two oceans. Three continents of opportunity.

The Zambia Lobito Railway (ZLR) is a c.830km greenfield rail line from Chingola, Zambia to Luacano in Angola, connecting to the existing Benguela line and the Zambia Railways network - thereby completing the Trans-African rail corridor.

This AFC-led development will establish a critical trade route for copper, critical minerals, agricultural products, petroleum and general cargo, connecting inland Southern African countries to international markets via the Port of Lobito on the Atlantic coast. The development is set to reduce transit times to 7 days, down from up to 16 days via alternative corridors.

  • Project Route - Luacano, Angola to Chingola, Zambia
  • Distance - c. 830km of new greenfield rail
  • Lead Developer - Africa Finance Corporation (AFC)
  • Transit Time - 6–7 days (vs. 16 days via alternatives)

The ZLR bridges the existing Benguela line in Angola and the Zambia Railways network, opening Atlantic and Indian Ocean trade routes.

Cargo & Capacity

Built for freight that builds nations.

Mining & Critical Minerals

Copper from the Zambian Copperbelt and critical minerals essential to the global energy transition - moving efficiently to Atlantic shipping routes.

Agriculture

Anchoring agricultural production targets across Angola and Zambia - opening new export markets for local farmers.

Petroleum

Reliable inland distribution of petroleum products to Southern Africa's industrial and consumer markets.

General Freight

Containerised and general cargo - manufactured goods, equipment and consumer products moving in both directions along the corridor.

ESIA Disclosure

The full Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) and Resettlement Action Plan (RAP) are being undertaken by Insuco.
