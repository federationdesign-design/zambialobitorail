Economic & Social Value

Trade routes that build economies.

Trade Connectivity

  • Reliable, efficient transport for copper, petroleum, agricultural products and general cargo.
  • Connects the Copperbelt to the Atlantic with 6–7 day transit times - outpacing routes via Dar es Salaam, Beira and Durban.

Economic Growth

  • Substantial construction employment plus long-term operational positions along the corridor.
  • Anchors agricultural production targets, opening new markets for Zambian and Angolan farmers.
  • US$3 billion in preliminary Net Economic Benefits driven by increased consumer surplus.

Environmental Standards

International best practice from day one.

The ZLR adheres to IFC Performance Standards covering environmental & social risk management, labour and working conditions, resource efficiency, community health and safety, land acquisition, resettlement, and biodiversity conservation.

The development framework also incorporates the AfDB Integrated Safeguards System, the Equator Principles, and World Bank Group EHS Guidelines. ESIA & RAP are being undertaken by Insuco.

  • IFC Performance Standards - Full alignment across all 8 standards
  • AfDB Integrated Safeguards System - Compliance with continental development bank framework
  • Equator Principles - Project finance risk management
  • World Bank EHS Guidelines - Industry-leading environmental, health & safety practices
