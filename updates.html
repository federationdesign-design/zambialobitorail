Project Updates

Latest News & Announcements

Track the progress of the Zambia Lobito Railway - from feasibility studies and intergovernmental agreements through to financial close.

September 2024

Feasibility Studies Completed

Comprehensive feasibility studies confirm the technical, commercial and economic viability of the Zambia Lobito Railway - including engineering, traffic, financial and economic modelling.

September 2024

Concession Agreements Executed

Concession Agreements signed with both governments, providing the legal framework for the 830km greenfield rail corridor and confirming the project's commercial structure.

April 2025

Intergovernmental Agreement Signed

Zambia and Angola formally executed the Intergovernmental Agreement, marking a critical step toward the development of the ZLR corridor and providing the binational legal framework for cross-border rail operations.

October 2025

Stage 1 CP Approval

Conditions Precedent (Stage 1) under the Zambia Concession Agreement satisfied.

Ongoing

Environmental & Social Studies

Detailed Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) and Resettlement Action Plan (RAP) underway, led by Insuco. Target disclosure: March 2026.

Q3 2026 (planned)

EPC Contractor Selection

EPC tender AN25162-0100R is open for civils & plate laying (Angola and Zambia) and signalling, systems & rolling stock.

Q3 2026

O&M Contracts Launched

Procurement launched for the Operations & Maintenance contractor.

Q3 2026

Financing Raise Launched

Commencement of the project financing raise process.

Q4 2027 (planned)

Financial Close

Equity and debt raise commencing Q2 2027, with financial close scheduled for Q4 2027 - enabling commencement of construction.
