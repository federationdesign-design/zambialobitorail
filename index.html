The Zambia Lobito Railway (ZLR)

The Zambia Lobito Railway (ZLR) is a c.830km greenfield rail line from Chingola, Zambia to Luacano in Angola, connecting to the existing Benguela line and the Zambia Railways network - thereby completing the Trans-African rail corridor.

This AFC-led development will establish a critical trade route for copper, critical minerals, agricultural products, petroleum and general cargo, connecting inland Southern African countries to international markets via the Port of Lobito on the Atlantic coast. The development is set to reduce transit times to 7 days, down from up to 16 days via alternative corridors.

About ZLR

Project Sponsor

Project Sponsor - Africa Finance Corporation

The Africa Finance Corporation (AFC) serves as lead developer for the ZLR project, in partnership with the Governments of Zambia and Angola. Established in 2007 as a Public Private Partnership of sovereign African states, AFC has an investment footprint across 36 African countries with 43 African member states plus 3 multilateral members.

ESIA Disclosure

The Zambia Lobito Railway project is being developed in line with comprehensive international environmental and social standards. The project adheres to:

  • IFC Performance Standards (Assessment & Management of E&S Risks; Labour & Working Conditions; Resource Efficiency & Pollution Prevention; Community Health, Safety & Security; Land Acquisition & Resettlement; Biodiversity Conservation & Sustainable Management of Living Natural Resources)
  • AfDB Integrated Safeguards System
  • Equator Principles
  • World Bank Group EHS Guidelines

The full ESIA and Resettlement Action Plan (RAP) are being undertaken by Insuco. Detailed disclosure documents will be made available here as studies progress (target: March 2026).
